The price

Two U1s. One for every day, one for the working fleet.

We are introducing the U1 in two models, because an everyday rider and a working fleet do not need the same bike. The U1-R is for everyday riders who buy for themselves, with premium finishes and lifestyle accessories for the commute, the errands and the weekend. The U1-F is for the working fleet, a simple, bare build set up to each fleet's spec and made to run shift after shift. Same frame and battery bay underneath, each one finished for the job it does.

How a rider gets the fleet price

  1. 01Ask your platform

    Tell the fleet or platform you ride for that you want a U1-F. If you ride for more than one, pick the one you buy through.

  2. 02The fleet approves you

    Each fleet decides which riders can buy through it. The fleet price is open to fleets with 100 or more U1s assigned.

  3. 03Pay the fleet price, keep the saving

    You pay the price set for that fleet, for a U1-F built to its spec. The saving against retail is yours.

Cost per working day₫

Run it against your own day

Cost per working day is energy, maintenance, depreciation and insurance, set against the petrol bike being ridden today. It moves with distance, load and shift pattern, which is why an average helps nobody. Both calculators start from your own numbers.

Rider economics → You own the bike and ride it yourself. Fuel saved per day, what a pack costs to charge, and how long the switch takes to pay back. Fleet economics → You run vehicles other people ride. Cost per working day across the fleet, uptime against a charging cycle, and what downtime is costing you now.
Audience

Who it is for

Built first for fleets that work all day, ready for riders who buy their own. The U1-F is set up to each fleet's spec, the U1-R is finished for everyday life. Whichever you ride, the vehicle stays our responsibility, not the swap network's.

01

Last mile delivery

Stop and start duty, loaded racks, riders handing over between shifts. 200 kg maximum payload, rider included, and mounts front, mid and rear.

02

B2B fleets

Emission inspection becomes mandatory in July 2027 and Hanoi is already piloting a low emission zone. The dates are public.

03

Multi fleet riders

You ride for whoever pays best this hour. The bike is yours, not the fleet's, and it runs on the swap network you choose.

04

Ride hailing

High daily kilometers on thin margins per trip. A swap under two minutes keeps the bike earning where a charge stops it.

05

Commuters

Two trips a day, plus errands and the school run. Run one pack or two, and carry a pack inside to charge, not the bike.

06

Adventure riders

Weekend distance outside the city, gear on the rack. 195 km maximum range, 140 to 160 km loaded, swap where you find one.
